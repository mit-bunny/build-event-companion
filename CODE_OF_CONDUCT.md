# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors should make participation respectful, welcoming, and free from harassment. Constructive disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, discriminatory language, intimidation, and disclosure of private information are not.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security reporting channel. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior threatens the safety or usefulness of the community.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.
